About AquaAlert

Water is one of our most valuable natural resources. Unnoticed leaking taps, damaged pipes and overflowing tanks can waste a large amount of water.

AquaAlert provides a digital form for recording suspected leaks, including their location, type and urgency. It also promotes responsible water usage.
